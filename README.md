The ONNX Community Meetup brings together developers, researchers, and industry practitioners working with or building on ONNX.
This event is an opportunity to share real-world experiences, showcase innovations, and discuss the future of the ONNX ecosystem

The schedules of the regular meetings of the Steering Committee, the working groups and the SIGs can be found [here](https://onnx.ai/calendar).

Community Meetups are held at least once a year. Content from previous community meetups are at:
