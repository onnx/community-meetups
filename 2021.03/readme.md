Learn more about the LF AI & Data Foundation [here](https://lfaidata.foundation/). **Examples of previous LF AI Days:** [LF AI Day Shanghai 2019](https://events19.linuxfoundation.cn/events/lf-ai-day-shanghai-2019), [LF AI Day Paris 2019](https://events19.linuxfoundation.org/events/lfaiday-paris-2019/), [Virtual LF AI Day EU 2020](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/virtual-lf-ai-day-eu/) # Ways the LF AI & Data Foundation Can Help Support Your Event The LF AI & Data Foundation can help support your event in many ways, including: 1. LF AI & Data Day event website creation - *Event Host to provide agenda and other content for website.* 2. Registration form creation on Cvent - *once launched, weekly registration reports will be sent to event host.* 3. Blog post and social posts announcing event once the website & registration form are launched. 4. Event graphic created to help promote the event. 5. Series of social posts on the LF AI & Data Twitter & LinkedIn pages leading up to the event. 6. Zoom meeting setup - *for virtual events.* 7. Pre-event email & post-event email sent to attendees via Cvent. 8. Post-event recap blog & social posts with links to the recordings & presentations. # Requirements for Hosting an LF AI & Data Day **If you are interested in hosting an LF AI & Data Day, please review the checklist below carefully and reach out to [events@lfaidata.foundation](mailto:events@lfaidata.foundation) with your request.** **Please include in your email request:** 1. The community organization(s)/members(s) who are planning to host the event. 2. Location and format (in person or virtual). 3. Target event date. 4. Primary host contact name and email for the event planning and overall execution. **IMPORTANT:** LF AI & Data Days require the LF AI & Data Foundation to budget for and find resourcing for event coordination support. Because of this, it is very important that you email [events@lfaidata.foundation](mailto:events@lfaidata.foundation) to confirm your interest and request to host an LF AI & Data Day. A response on availability to resource the event on the requested date will be provided to you shortly after. **A minimum of 2 months lead time is required in order to plan for the resourcing required as well as to properly promote the event.** We look forward to working with you on an LF AI & Data Day! # Event Checklist **The checklist below provides a detailed list of deliverables that must be completed by both the Event Host and LF AI & Data in order to successfully plan an LF AI & Data Day.** Once the Event Host has requested to host an LF AI & Data Day, and LF AI & Data staff have approved the event, this checklist page should be copied and used for the specific LF AI & Data Day, and should be linked to the specific event wiki page. You will be assigned an LF AI & Data staff person who will be your primary contact throughout the planning process.
